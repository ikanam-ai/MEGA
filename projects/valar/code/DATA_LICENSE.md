# Data licensing

The MIT License in this repository applies to source code only.

VALAR's item banks are derived from documented third-party Russian-language
datasets, including TAPE and the inappropriate/sensitive-topics collections.
Source texts retain the terms and attribution requirements of their respective
upstream datasets. Model-generated annotations and outputs may additionally be
subject to the relevant model or service terms.

The OSF record is the authoritative distribution point for the research data,
provenance notes, and human-validation artifacts. Users are responsible for
checking the upstream terms before redistributing derived text collections.
